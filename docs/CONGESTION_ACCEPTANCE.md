# Congestion acceptance contract v1 — proposed engineering targets

These are **product acceptance targets**, not measured Toronto standards. Pin the network, code commits, seeds, fleet, fixed signal plans, demand profile and target zone before testing. For the current district use the interior Main/King/Queen arterial lanes (6.273 lane-km), excluding fringe stubs, sidewalks, cycle-only lanes and internal junction lanes. Report the whole network separately. Declare directions explicitly; do not mix a peak-direction target with a bidirectional measurement. The present audit uses all directions on the three arterials. Include connecting collector lanes in a declared gridlock component when they are needed to form the blocked cycle, while reporting the arterial subset separately. A localized incident may use a predeclared zone of at least 2 lane-km across two connected corridors/four physical junctions; do not select a small dense patch after seeing results and label the entire network gridlocked.

## Targets

| Achieved class | Density, veh/km/lane | Standstill-footprint storage use | Spatial body occupancy | Speed distribution, km/h | Queue and duration evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Regular rush | 40–75 | about 30–60% | about 20–40% | median 8–20; p90 ≤35 | ≥8 vehicles / ≥50 m on at least two approaches; recurrent stop-and-go; minimum density for ≥240/300 s |
| Severe congestion | 85–115 | ≥65%, usually 65–90% | ≥40% | median ≤8; p90 ≤20; ≥55% halted | queues reach ≥75% of two consecutive physical blocks on a connected corridor, with receiving-lane blockage evidence for ≥120 s; density/storage targets for ≥240/300 s |
| Actual gridlock in declared zone | ≥110, bounded by actual fleet jam density | ≥85% | ≥50% | median ≤1; p90 ≤5; ≥85% halted | blocked directed cycle through ≥4 junctions, across ≥2 corridors, persists ≥120 s while legal signal cycles continue; outflow across that component ≤10% of its matched nonlocked reference; density/storage targets for ≥240/300 s |

Ranges guide matching; a low requested tier that overshoots must report the higher achieved tier. Low speed, one red-light queue, high offered demand, insertion backlog, or a screenshot alone never qualifies as gridlock. Ordinary rush must move and discharge; an unexplained nearly stationary sparse network is a failure to investigate. Severe congestion can have substantial throughput. A deliberately blocked stress case is labeled **stress**, not calibrated rush hour.

For a 4.75 m car, standstill packing at 2–5 m gaps is 148–103 veh/km/lane. Use the actual mixed fleet and gaps to check feasibility; buses/trucks reduce car-equivalent capacity. Target at least 90% of stable, same-lane passenger-car following gaps in 2–5 m, median approximately 2.5–3.5 m, and no unintended contacts. Do not force spacing with speed overrides, reduced safety checks, low time headways, teleport insertion, or aggressive lane changing. Existing minGap draws can be below 2 m; any bounded-profile change belongs to the scenario/driver owner and needs a safety sweep. In SUMO, `getLeader` reports gap after subtracting the follower's minGap: add it back to obtain bumper-to-bumper distance. Sample after both vehicles have stood ≥5 s, exclude lane-change/side-by-side cases, and retain outliers.

## Required measurements

- **Density:** number of motor vehicles on the selected noninternal lanes / their summed length in km. Include empty lanes in the denominator. Separate cyclists/pedestrians; do not count junction vehicles as road storage.
- **Spatial occupancy:** sum of vehicle body lengths clipped to the selected lane boundaries / lane-metres; disclose junction-boundary undercount. This is a lane-length measure, not two-dimensional asphalt area or a time-occupancy detector reading. Record native detector occupancy separately with its time interval if used.
- **Standstill-footprint utilization:** sum of native vehicle length + native standstill gap divided by lane-metres. This estimates used jam storage, not actual moving headways. Report the actual fleet and both measures; never call it the proportion of road covered by cars.
- **Queue:** native lane-area jam length or a connected-lane queue extent in metres and vehicles, low-speed threshold 1 m/s, persistence ≥10 s, intervehicle threshold ≤10 m. Trace across main/bay pieces without counting each piece as a block. A queue tail within 10 m of the physical upstream junction is a spillback candidate. Confirm that it blocks an upstream movement during ≥10 s of eligible green and that the receiving lane cannot accept vehicles. Adjacent queues are a proxy, not causal proof.
- **Blocked cycle:** build a directed graph of proven blocked receiving movements. Record junction IDs, signal states, receiving lanes and persistence; require a cycle of ≥4 physical junctions. Check component cut outflow, not total district completions that may occur elsewhere.
- **Accounting:** sampled requests, external holding queue, submitted, inserted (native departures), current SUMO pending, route failures, cancellations/discards, completed trips and removals/teleports. Expected-rate integrals are separate from integer requests. Report interval deltas and t=0 baselines; warm-up cannot inflate five-minute throughput.
- **Stop-and-go:** vehicle-ID transitions from stopped (<0.1 m/s) to moving (>2 m/s) and back; require ≥20 distinct motor vehicles with ≥2 stop/start episodes inside the observation for rush/severe. Retain speed p10/p50/p90 at 1 Hz and travel-time distributions.

## Exact release tests

1. **Input/seed lock:** seeds 7/42/99 must all pass each tier; add held-out 314/2026 before a demonstration release. Repeat seed 42 twice and compare native trajectories and metrics under identical inputs. Never average away a failed safety seed.
2. **Warm-up:** run a realistic peak plateau of at least 1,800 simulated seconds, with the 300-second observation a slice inside it. Warm up at full computation speed, initially 300–600 s, bounded at 1,200 s. The requested minimum density/storage must hold for the final two complete conventional signal cycles before t=0 and at t=0. Demand intensity stays at least 90% of the intended plateau throughout warm-up tail and observation. On timeout return `target_unmet`, not a stronger achieved class. Do not compress rise/peak/fall into 300 s.
3. **Observation:** exactly 300 s after warm-up; 1 Hz samples; pass the table's density/storage/speed/queue requirements on every seed. Retain the causal spillback/cycle evidence. Require final-60-second density to be at least 80% of first-60-second density for sustained severe/gridlock, absent a documented release event.
4. **Physics/safety:** zero unintended native vehicle/vehicle and vehicle/person collision records, including warm-up; junction checks and warnings enabled; zero invalid active routes; legal bicycle lanes; full native signal clearance. Queue spacing must pass without disabling safety. Distinguish unique collision pairs, repeated observations, insertion versus movement conflicts, pedestrian jams and collisions.
5. **No artificial rescue or hidden loss:** normal/severe must have zero teleports. A gridlock stress test must explicitly disclose and disable automatic deadlock rescue for its observation if persistence is to be tested; this is not a collision-check exemption. With the current 240 s teleport rescue, do not claim an unassisted stationary gridlock state. Use explicit external demand holding rather than silently discarding at 180 s. Enforce integer conservation: external_start + requests = external_end + submitted + explicit_route_rejections + explicit_cancellations; native_retained_start + newly_loaded = native_retained_end + arrived + discarded + explicitly_removed, where retained is a disjoint partition of active, pending, teleporting and other registered unfinished vehicles. Use interval deltas of native cumulative counters; do not add cumulative loaded totals to active populations. Include scenario-generated vehicles in the native ledger and publish demand categories separately.
6. **Compatibility/performance:** after approval, test an isolated combined candidate with all scenario tests, API/WebSocket startup/restart, closure restoration and emergency dispatch at dense load. No source schema breaks. Achieve at least real-time SUMO (4 steps/s at dt=.25) with all checks and reporting enabled on the target laptop. Frontend production builds: verify both cameras at 800/1200/1500 native vehicles, median ≥50 FPS, p95 frame ≤33 ms and <1% frames >50 ms; keep native frames at 4–20 Hz and show actual achieved simulation speed. These are new targets, not results already established by the 460-vehicle frontend report.
